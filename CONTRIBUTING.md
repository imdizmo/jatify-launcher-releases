# Contributing to Jatify Launcher

Thanks for wanting to help. Bug reports and ideas are welcome.

Jatify Launcher is one of the Jatify tools (Groundwork, Asset Shelf, Prop Generator and the Jatify Launcher). They share one
look and the same way of working, so this guide is nearly the same in each repository.

The launcher's source is private, so this repository takes issues but not pull requests.

## Reporting a bug

Open an issue and say:

- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- the version (it is in the status bar or under About) and whether you are on Linux or Windows;
- the log or a screenshot when you have one.

The app's Details (its last error) or your `apps.json` overlay helps most when the problem is with one app. Only attach
files you are allowed to share, and remove tokens and personal paths first.

**Security problems do not go in a public issue.** Report them privately through the repository's Security tab
(**Report a vulnerability**).

## Suggesting a change

Open an issue. Say what you want the launcher to do and why.

## Getting help

Ask in an issue.
